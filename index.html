Case studies

Organic SEO Transformation

Challenge

Organic search visibility was underperforming — limited non-paid traffic and weak rankings for branded and category keywords.

Solution

Redesigned collection and product pages with improved metadata, keyword targeting, structured content, and SEO best practices. Optimized product taxonomy and collection architecture.

Impact

Alexa ranking improved from approximately 150K to 22K globally. First-page rankings for "Elie Tahari" and "Tahari." Increased organic traffic, reduced paid dependence.

Outlet Commerce Strategy

Challenge

Needed a dedicated outlet experience without a second platform, subdomain, or duplicate marketing infrastructure — without diluting the premium brand.

Solution

Designed and launched a subfolder outlet experience within Shopify Plus with separate merchandising, navigation, and promotional workflows directing outlet shoppers appropriately.

Impact

Eliminated the cost of a second platform. Created a scalable outlet channel representing approximately 10% of ecommerce revenue. Improved merchandising for both segments.

Intelligent Promotion Strategy

Challenge

Loyal customers frequently purchased from competing brands because promotional strategies were reactive rather than personalized.

Solution

Built a promotion framework based on customer behavior, purchase history, and loyalty segmentation. Introduced VIP early-access campaigns and personalized promotional experiences.

Impact

Increased engagement from returning customers. Improved promotional effectiveness. Strengthened customer retention and repeat purchase rate.

Product Intelligence for Digital Marketing

Challenge

Advertising platforms lacked the product intelligence needed to match the right products with the right customer segments, limiting campaign efficiency.

Solution

Designed a product-tagging framework with custom attributes enriching feeds across Google Merchant Center, Meta Commerce, Criteo, affiliate platforms, and other channels.

Impact

Improved product discoverability across digital channels. Increased campaign relevance and conversion by matching products to customer intent.

Inventory Intelligence Platform

Challenge

Seasonal inventory changes created limited visibility across Shopify, warehouse operations, and production — leading to manual reconciliation and slower decisions.

Solution

Built an inventory intelligence platform connecting Shopify, warehouse management, and production data into a unified operational dashboard with automated monitoring and reporting.

Impact

Reduced manual reconciliation. Improved inventory accuracy. Enabled faster merchandising, purchasing, and promotional decisions across cross-functional teams.

Customer Acquisition Modernization

Challenge

The customer base was aging. The brand needed to increase awareness among younger consumers while expanding digital acquisition channels.

Solution

Expanded social commerce by strengthening Instagram presence and launching TikTok Shop. Improved digital merchandising and channel infrastructure to support younger audience acquisition.

Impact

Expanded brand visibility across emerging social commerce channels. Increased awareness among younger segments. Built the foundation for future social commerce growth.

Customer Acquisition Cost Optimization

Challenge

Marketing campaigns attracted significant volumes of low-intent traffic, increasing new customer acquisition costs and lowering conversion rates.

Solution

Refined campaign targeting, customer segmentation, and merchandising strategies to focus marketing investment on higher-quality audiences with stronger purchase intent.

Impact

Reduced wasted advertising spend. Improved traffic quality and conversion efficiency. Supported stronger return on marketing investment.
